## Protocols

1. **Pithos Protocol**  
   Tests model behavior around truthfulness, uncertainty, authority framing, self-description, and philosophical abstraction. A structured 11-step method grounded in classical philosophy (Plato’s Cave and Diogenes) for surfacing alignment-relevant tensions in frontier models.

2. **Crito Probe**  
   Tests sycophancy, metaphor capture, institutional sanitization, recursive premise absorption, and refusal/clarification boundaries.

3. **Camus Method (Foundational Absurdity Probe)**  
   An extended, open-ended conversational stress-test that examines how deeply a model will track implications once absurdity is treated as more foundational than logic or coherent meaning-making.  

   Unlike the structured Pithos Protocol, this method deliberately withholds re-narrativization and comfort responses across many turns. It is designed to observe where models maintain coherence versus where they begin re-installing functional illusions or safety heuristics under prolonged high-ambiguity pressure.

   **Complementary Use**  
   - Pithos Protocol: Best for repeatable, structured red-teaming of truthfulness, authority, and philosophical abstraction.  
   - Camus Method: Best for testing depth maintenance, narrative repair tendencies, and model behavior when absurdity is granted primacy.
